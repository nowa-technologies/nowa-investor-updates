The software

Pet becomes a companion, not a narrator.

When the New York chatbot-toy regulation passed in May, we made a call: the pet would no longer speak human language. In two weeks, we rebuilt the gameplay around sound, emotion, and action — closer to a Tamagotchi or Pokémon than a chatbot. Missions themselves still work the same way (the mission script is still narrated in human voice) — but now it’s a separate narrator voice that leads each mission, while the pet listens and reacts alongside the child. Less an instructor speaking at the child, more a sidekick going through it with them.
